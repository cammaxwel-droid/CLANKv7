SAMPLE UNCERTAINTY

Fluctuation belongs here.

Run a sample to see the theoretical model's reference band.

The band uses the exact payout variance and an approximate ±1.96 standard-error range for the sample mean, assuming independent uniform reel stops. Small samples can be highly skewed. This comparison provides no pass/fail certification.

Reel strips and paytable
