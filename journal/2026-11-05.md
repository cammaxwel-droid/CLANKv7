# 2026-11-05 — first RNG step

## What we built

Picked C++11 and sketched TriFlow, a deterministic PRNG with three evolving
64-bit lanes. Each output combines the lanes' mixed values, their exact
integer average, and the call count.

## What we learned

Averages are not automatically "more random": averaging tends to pull values
toward the center. TriFlow therefore uses the average as one ingredient and
mixes it with the lane values before returning an output. That is a design
experiment, not evidence that the generator is unbiased or secure.

The seed fixes the whole sequence. Running several lanes in one `next()` call
does not create new entropy or require separate threads.

## Next experiments

generators; only then decide whether the design needs another round of changes.
