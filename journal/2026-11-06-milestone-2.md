## What changed and why

seeds, state and raw outputs. `BigInt.asUintN` makes 64-bit wrapping explicit.
The average adds three wide integers before division, matching Python's exact
average and C++'s quotient/remainder approach. Separate boundary tests check
maximum values that would fail if the sum were prematurely wrapped.

identifier `triflow-v1` and `RawRng` declarations. Seeds must be canonical
lowercase 16-digit text. State stays in per-instance closures and the returned
